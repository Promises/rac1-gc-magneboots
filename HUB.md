# Going Commando Magneboots

Going Commando's Gravity Boots in place of Ratchet & Clank's Magneboots.

[![Going Commando Magneboots in game (video)](docs/video-thumbnail.jpg)](https://youtu.be/TKqdbXuQDlA)

[Watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/TKqdbXuQDlA) (14 s, with sound): running and fighting upside
down on a magnetic track.

## What it does

In Ratchet & Clank the Magneboots have a slow magnet walk of their own: Ratchet plods along, stops
to turn when you point the stick far away, X is only a hop on the spot, and his weapons and quick
select are locked. Going Commando has none of that: its magnetic surfaces are plain ground with
their own gravity.

This mod gives the Magneboots Going Commando's way. On magnetic surfaces Ratchet walks, runs, jumps
and fights as on any ground, and a jump, a double jump or a step off an edge comes back down onto
the surface. The item keeps its name.

## How to use it

Nothing new to learn: on a magnetic surface, play as you would on any ground. Run with the stick,
jump and double jump with X, and use your weapons and quick select.

**Edge assist.** Running makes a narrow magnetic track easy to run off, so the mod helps: running
at an edge, Ratchet is turned along it. To step off on purpose, keep pushing straight out over the
edge for about a third of a second. Jumps are never touched.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Edge assist | On | On, Off | On narrow magnetic tracks, running at an edge turns Ratchet along it; keep pushing out over it and he steps off. |

## Requirements

- **OpenRAC with mod interface 1.22 or later.** Until a release has it, that is the launcher's
  **Dev** update channel (Settings). On an older OpenRAC the launcher holds the mod back rather
  than install something that cannot load.
- **Ratchet & Clank**, PAL and NTSC-U.
- No other mods needed.

## Compatibility and known issues

No conflicts with other mods are declared, and no known issues are listed.
